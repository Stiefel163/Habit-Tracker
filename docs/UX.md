# Comeback: design notes

## Goal

Make real productive actions feel as rewarding as progressing in a game, without turning life into a spreadsheet. The app tracks only actions you directly control: training, swimming, Spanish, publishing videos, driving-theory sheets and focused work.

## Principles

1. **Action → XP → progression.** Every log gives skill XP and player XP right away, with a short reward moment.
2. **Fast logging.** Each quick log takes one or two taps plus, for Spanish and videos, one sentence.
3. **One reflective question, where it matters.** *"What got better today?"* for Spanish and *"What got better with this video?"* for each published video. Over time they form a scrollable record of improvement, which doubles as the cookie jar.
4. **Input vs. output.** Hours of work are Focus (with a category). Published videos are the output, the Road to 100.
5. **Forgiving streak.** The Spanish streak has a Streak Shield, so one bad day doesn't end it, but missing twice does. Totals are never lost.
6. **Weekly over daily.** Rest days are neutral. Weekly missions give stars; nothing is subtracted.
7. **No manipulation.** One workout, swim and Spanish day per day count. Focus XP comes from total time, so splitting a session earns nothing extra.
8. **Achievements only from real logs.** There are no lifestyle quests.

## Ideas behind it

- **Volume first** (Hormozi's "more, better, new"): weekly targets and the Road to 100 reward doing the reps.
- **Iteration** (MrBeast): one improvement per video, written down.
- **Compare with yesterday, not with others** (Peterson): the "what got better" entries.
- **Proof on hard days** (Goggins' cookie jar): your own wins next to your improvements.
- **Vision** (Kanye): one line on Home about why you're doing this.
- **Mastery as a long road:** focus hours per category toward ~1,000 hours, shown as a rule of thumb, not a promise.
